❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers about claim success probability

How accurate is this calculator? +

Our calculator provides estimates based on industry-standard factors that influence claim success. While we can't guarantee outcomes, we use weighted averages from insurance industry data to give you a realistic probability range.

What's the most important factor for claim success? +

Evidence strength typically has the highest impact (25-35% weight depending on claim type), followed by policy coverage and documentation. The exact importance varies by claim type - legal claims value witness support more than warranty claims, for example.

Why does my claim type affect the base rate? +

Different claims have different approval patterns. Health insurance claims have higher base approval rates (60%) than legal claims (30%) due to regulatory requirements. We adjust for these industry norms automatically.

How should I interpret a "Low Confidence" result? +

A result below 30% suggests significant obstacles. Focus on the improvement tips provided - often strengthening evidence or clarifying policy coverage can dramatically improve your chances.

Does claim amount affect success probability? +

Our current model doesn't factor in amount as approval rates don't always correlate with dollar values. However, extremely high claims may require additional documentation - consider this when rating your "Documentation" factor.

How recent should my incident be? +

Timeliness matters! Most policies require claims within:
- Auto: 30 days
- Home: 1 year
- Health: 6-12 months
Adjust the "Timeliness" slider accordingly.

Can I save my calculation results? +

Currently, results aren't saved automatically. We recommend screenshotting your results page or noting the breakdown for reference. Future versions may include save/print functionality.

Why is the maximum probability 95%? +

We cap at 95% because even seemingly perfect claims can face unexpected issues (policy exclusions, administrative errors, etc.). This prevents overconfidence while still recognizing strong cases.

How often is the calculation model updated? +

We review our algorithms quarterly using the latest industry data. Significant regulatory changes (like new insurance laws) trigger immediate updates.

Is my claim data stored or shared? +

No - all calculations happen in your browser. We don't store claim details or results (see Privacy Policy). For education/research, we only collect anonymous aggregate statistics.
